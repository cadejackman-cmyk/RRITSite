How we work

Four things we actually believe

Prevention beats heroics

Most IT disasters were preventable. Catching a failing drive or a stopped backup early is far less dramatic than an emergency repair - and a lot cheaper for you.

We'll tell you when you don't need something

We've talked clients out of cloud moves that would've cost more and delivered less, and out of replacing hardware that had another year in it. Advice you can't trust is worth nothing.

Local means someone can show up

Most problems are fixed faster remotely, wherever you are. When one needs hands on the equipment, our engineers come out across northern and southern Utah - and you are never routed to a queue in another time zone.

We say what's actually true

No company can make you HIPAA compliant. No product makes you immune to ransomware. We'd rather give you a short list you'll act on than a long one that looks impressive.
