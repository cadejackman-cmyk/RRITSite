Why it matters who is running your IT company

Most IT companies that support pharmacies learned the vocabulary second-hand. They know a label printer is important because a pharmacy told them so, and they know the software is called PioneerRx because it says so on the screen.

Dallas learned it as a pharmacist. He knows what a stopped queue costs, what an antivirus exclusion has to look like before a fill can print, and why a five-minute reboot at the wrong hour is not a five-minute problem. That is the difference between an IT company that supports pharmacies and one that understands them.

It also means our advice is not sales-led. When a pharmacy asks whether it needs something, the answer comes from someone who has worked the other side of the counter.
