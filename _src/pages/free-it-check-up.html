What we look at

Four things, honestly assessed

Not a sales audit dressed up as a favour. A straight look at the equipment you depend on.

Computers

Age, condition, what is still under warranty, what is running an operating system that stopped getting security updates, and what will need replacing this year.

Servers

What is running on them, how they are patched, whether anything critical is sitting on hardware old enough to be a risk on its own.

Network

Wi-Fi coverage and the equipment behind it, the firewall, how remote access is handled, and who can reach what.

Backups

Whether they are running, whether anyone has tested a restore, and whether ransomware could reach them. This is the one that surprises people most often.
