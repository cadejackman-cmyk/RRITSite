Questions to ask any IT company in Sandy

Whether you hire us or someone else, these are the questions that separate a real IT partner from a phone number:

  • Who comes on site, and how fast? "We'll send someone" is not an answer. Ask where they are based and what a typical visit looks like.
  • When did you last test a restore? A backup nobody has restored from is a hope, not a backup. Ask when they last proved one works.
  • Who owns the passwords and the documentation? It should be you. If you left, could the next company pick it up the same day?
  • Who is watching the security alerts at night? Security software that alerts to an empty room does not stop ransomware.
  • What is billed on top of the monthly fee? Ask for it in writing, before you sign.

We are happy to answer every one of them. The free IT check-up is a good place to start. You get a written report on what you have, what is aging and what is at risk, and it is yours to keep whether you hire us or not.
