Common questions

Can you move us from Gmail or an old email server?

Yes, we do it regularly. Email, calendars, contacts and files come across, usually over a weekend, and we keep the old system running in parallel until we're sure nothing was missed.

Isn't Microsoft already backing up our email?

Not the way most people assume. Microsoft keeps the service running, but if something is deleted and sits past their retention window, it's gone. Your data is your responsibility - see backup and recovery.

Our invoices keep going to customers' spam. Can you fix it?

Usually yes, and usually quickly. It's normally three missing records on your domain name. Setting them up correctly fixes delivery and stops anyone forging emails from your address.

We already have 365. Can you just look after it?

Yes. We start with a free review of your licenses, security settings and email setup, and hand you the findings either way.
