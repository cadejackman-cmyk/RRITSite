The one change that matters most

Right now, everything on your network can probably talk to everything else - the guest phone in reception, the security cameras, the card reader and the server holding your customer records.

So one hacked device has a clear run at all of it - and cameras and printers get hacked constantly, because nobody updates them. We split the network into separate zones so they can't reach each other. It's mostly configuration rather than new equipment, and it's the biggest security win most businesses can get.

Questions

Common questions

Do we have to replace all our equipment?

Usually not. We'll tell you what's genuinely holding you back versus what's just older but fine, and phase any replacements.

Our Wi-Fi drops in part of the building. Is that fixable?

Nearly always - and it's rarely because the equipment is too weak. It's usually placement or interference from a neighboring business. The fix is normally moving and retuning what you have.

Can you connect our other locations together?

Yes. Offices, warehouses and jobsite trailers can all be linked so your systems work the same at each one.

Will this disrupt the business?

Very little. We build the new setup alongside the old one and move things across in small groups, mostly outside working hours.
