Where we go in the Salt Lake Valley

Being based in Sandy puts most of the valley within a short drive. We regularly work on site in Salt Lake City itself, and across Sandy, Draper, Midvale, Murray, Cottonwood Heights, Holladay, South Jordan, West Jordan, Riverton, Taylorsville, West Valley City and Bluffdale.

Most issues never need a visit. Remote support starts within minutes, and the majority of everyday problems are sorted the same day without anyone getting in a truck. The office location matters for the rest: a failed server, a network that needs re-cabling, a new office fit-out, or a machine that will not boot.

The businesses we work with here

We are not a general help desk that takes anything. The Salt Lake Valley work is concentrated in a few industries where we already know what breaks:

  • Pharmacies. Independent and closed-door pharmacies where the dispensing system, label printers and HIPAA obligations all have to keep working. This is our deepest specialty.
  • Construction and manufacturing. Jobsite connectivity, shop floor networks and crews who need access from wherever they are that week.
  • Property management. Leasing offices, building Wi-Fi and camera networks across multiple properties.
  • Finance and professional services. Small offices with real compliance obligations and no appetite for downtime.

If you run several sites around the valley, that is where this works best. Every location gets built the same way and monitored together, so whichever engineer picks up your call already knows how your setup works.

Why local matters for IT support

Plenty of IT companies will support a Salt Lake City business remotely from another state. That works right up until it does not. The difference shows on the day something physical is wrong, when the answer is either an engineer in a vehicle or a shipping label and three days of waiting. It also shows in the smaller things: knowing the building, knowing which internet providers serve your street, and answering the phone in your time zone.

Questions

Common questions

Do you come on site in Salt Lake City?

Yes. Our office is in Sandy, so Salt Lake City and the rest of the valley are a short drive. Most problems are fixed remotely within minutes, and we come out when a fault needs hands on the equipment.

How quickly do you respond?

Remote work starts within minutes, and most everyday issues are resolved the same day. You are never routed to a queue in another time zone.

How much does IT support cost in Salt Lake City?

We quote one flat monthly fee after a free check-up, because the honest number depends on how many people and machines you have and what condition it is all in. Our guide to what small business IT support costs explains how the pricing models work and what moves the number.

We have offices in both northern and southern Utah. Can you cover both?

Yes, and it is a common setup for us. We have engineers based in southern Utah, near St George, so both ends of the state are covered by the same team with the same documentation. See our St George IT support page.

Do you work with small businesses, or only larger companies?

Small businesses are most of what we do. The usual fit is somewhere between five and a hundred people, often with more than one location.
