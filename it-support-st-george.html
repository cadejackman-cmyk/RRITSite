Where we go in Washington County

From the Washington office, most of the county is a short drive. We work on site in St George, Washington City, Hurricane, Ivins, Santa Clara, La Verkin, Toquerville and Leeds.

Most problems never need a visit at all. Remote support starts within minutes and handles the large majority of everyday issues the same day. Having people nearby matters for the rest: a server that will not come back up, a network that needs physical work, an office move, or new equipment that has to be set up and tested in place.

Why Southern Utah businesses end up calling us

The usual story we hear is one of two things. Either the business is on a remote-only contract with a company hundreds of miles away, and nobody has ever seen the equipment. Or they are relying on one local person who is good but is one person, which works until that person is on vacation, sick, or standing in someone else's server room.

Both are fine until the day they are not. What changes with a proper managed IT contract is that the monitoring, patching, backups and security are running whether or not anyone remembered to think about them, and there is more than one person who knows how your systems are put together.

The businesses we work with here

  • Pharmacies. Independent, long-term care and closed-door pharmacies. Dispensing software, label printers, HIPAA technical safeguards and a counter that cannot afford to stop. This is our deepest specialty.
  • Construction and manufacturing. Jobsite internet, shop floor networks, and crews who need access from wherever they are working.
  • Property management. Leasing offices, resident Wi-Fi and camera networks across multiple properties.
  • Professional services and medical offices. Small teams carrying real compliance obligations with no tolerance for downtime.

One team, both ends of the state

We also run an office in Sandy, in the Salt Lake Valley. If you have sites at both ends of Utah, they get built the same way, documented in the same place and monitored together, so you are not managing two different IT relationships. See our Salt Lake City IT support page for that side.

Questions

Common questions

Do you actually come on site in St George?

Yes. Our engineers live in Washington County, a few minutes from St George, so we cover it on site when a fault needs hands on the equipment. Most issues are resolved remotely within minutes.

Are you a local company or a national one with a St George number?

Local. RedRock IT is a Utah company with offices in Washington and Sandy. You are not routed to a call center in another time zone.

How much does IT support cost in St George?

One flat monthly fee, quoted after a free check-up, because the real number depends on how many people and machines you have and what state everything is in. Our guide to small business IT support costs walks through the pricing models and what pushes the number up or down.

Do you support pharmacies in Southern Utah?

Yes, and it is what we are known for. We support the systems around your dispensing software, work directly with your software vendor, and sign a business associate agreement as standard. See pharmacy IT support.

We only have a handful of computers. Are we too small?

Probably not. We work with businesses from about five people upward. Below that, hourly support sometimes makes more sense, and we will tell you if that is the honest answer for your situation.
