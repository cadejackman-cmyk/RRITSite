Your computers and servers check in with us around the clock. A failing hard drive gets replaced on a Tuesday visit instead of dying on a Monday morning.

Questions

Common questions

How much does it cost?

This page describes our flat monthly plan - a fixed fee based on how many people and devices you have, so day-to-day support never generates a surprise invoice. If you'd rather just call us when something breaks, we also work pay as you go, billed hourly with no monthly commitment. How pricing works. If you are comparing providers, our guide to IT support pricing sets out what should be in a monthly fee and what gets billed on top.

Can you work with the IT person we already have?

Yes, that's common. They keep the work they know best; we take monitoring, updates, security and after-hours cover.

What if something breaks at 9pm?

Our monitoring alerts us regardless of the hour, and most overnight issues are fixed remotely before your staff arrive. If you genuinely run evenings or weekends, we build that into the agreement.

Can you take over from our current provider without downtime?

Almost always. We get set up alongside them first and only switch once we can prove everything works.
