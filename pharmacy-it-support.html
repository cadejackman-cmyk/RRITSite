Being straight about HIPAA

No IT company can make you HIPAA compliant - compliance also covers your policies, training and paperwork. What we handle is the technology half: encryption, individual logins, two-factor, access records, tested backups and a separated network, documented so you can show your work.

Questions

Common questions

Do you support our pharmacy software?

We support everything it depends on - the computers, network, printers, scanners and backups - whichever system you run, and we work alongside your software vendor rather than around them.

What if we go down in the middle of the day?

A pharmacy that can't dispense is an emergency, not a ticket in a queue. We start immediately - remotely within minutes, and on-site in Northern Utah if it needs hands on equipment.

Will you sign a business associate agreement?

Yes, as standard. Any vendor touching systems that hold patient data should.

We have several locations. Can you cover them all?

Yes, and that's where this works best. Every site gets built the same way and monitored together, so any of our engineers already knows how yours is set up.
